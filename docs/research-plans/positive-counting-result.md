arithmetic amplitude matrices and coherent source relations are retained.
Additional reference preparations agree with the ordinary physical state

The unnormalized references D_E(h) have positive Taylor coefficients.
The normalized rho_E(h) are Poisson mixtures; their ordinary Taylor

profile is what makes the operational boundary reliable under continuation.

## How the blockers are addressed

The failed full tracial continuation is replaced by a specified STATE. Its
expectations are positive on all matrix amplifications and all mixed words.
The full arithmetic register and all coherent paths are retained, so the
missing relative Galois sectors and multiplication leave/return paths are
available. All-degree reference/tower identities use actual J/V maps and
do not divide by the extension degree. The finite-profile theorem supplies
one consistent rule for mixed asymptotic probabilities and conditioning.

These changes do not make the old obstructions false. They identify a
positive theory that avoids their hypotheses. The ordinary trace still
normalizes physical CP channels; the new reference need not be tracial or
Fourier invariant away from the arithmetic comparison point.

## What has not disappeared

The characteristic, field tables, cyclotomic phase, register dimensions and
some individual endpoint probabilities remain explicit data. For example,
quadratic nonzero-endpoint purities differ at p=2 and p=3. The counting
specialization is therefore not an intrinsic characteristic-one field or
a characteristic-independent full operator category.

The new rule also does not preserve naive polynomial point counts for every
coupled constraint. Copying a rare input and independently preparing two
rare inputs have different orders. Reference states are transported through
coordinate changes, not reset to independent target references. Exact
finite-h probabilities and arbitrary parameter-dependent angle operations
are outside the finite-profile equivalence theorem.

MIX-ALL remains CONJECTURE because identification with the earlier varying
angle construction and its stronger combined requirements has not been
established. LIM-SIGNED and more intrinsic characteristic-forgetting
constructions remain research directions. None is used to prove this theory.
