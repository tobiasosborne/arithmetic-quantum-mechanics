## 1. The intended result and the user's final clarification

Seek arithmetic symplectic objects and processes C_p, with a representation
R_p into positive quantum systems Q_p, sufficiently rich to form independent
tensors, collective assemblies, finite sums, and their actual experiments.
Use this represented arithmetic to construct a nontrivial p-to-one quantum
boundary with an arithmetic Frobenius acting as a CP process.

The full composition structure is required BEFORE specialization. The user
explicitly clarified that BOTH tensor-like and direct-sum-like composition
may disappear at the boundary. Neither a conventional C_1, nor total tensor
or sum operations on Q_1, is an acceptance condition. At first, Q_1 may name
only the collection of surviving quantum realizations and distinguished
processes. Determine its intrinsic structure from the construction.

The order of work is therefore:

    arithmetic constituents -> represented composite experiments
                            -> boundary quantum remnants and Frobenius.

Study the boundary of R_t(X assembled with Y) directly. Do not require it
to be reconstructible by assembling separate boundary images of X and Y.
Keep the finite arithmetic construction that produced a survivor as
provenance even when its assembly operation has no endpoint counterpart.

The spectral direction is a Frobenius/scaling realization related to
arithmetic zeta functions, with the Riemann spectral picture as the long-term
target discussed with the user. It guides the retained data and later trace
questions; it is not an asserted consequence of the present finite models.

This plan replaces the earlier priority of demanding identical predictions
at different primes. Characteristic-dependent responses can be evaluations
of a common structure. The actual interpolation and every retained
characteristic label must still be stated explicitly.

## 3. Keep the prelimit constructions distinct

The first draft must give these different operations different types.

| Construction before the limit | Data to preserve before any quotient |
|---|---|
| Independent quantum tensor | Spatial tensor algebras, product preparations, arbitrary allowed entangled inputs, and every instrument outcome |
| Collective arithmetic assembly | A composite arithmetic object and its full observable system, including observables outside the embedded local tensor algebra |
| Classical tagged sum | Block algebras A direct-sum B, branch probabilities, routing, and histories |
| Coherent additive sum | Source Hom blocks between summands and their represented off-diagonal operators, wherever the source actually supplies them |
| Projection/code selection | Named positive corner, its preparation weight, retained success/failure maps, and any necessary graded profile |

For collective assembly the existing prototype is a possibly proper map
End(X) tensor End(Y) -> End(X assembled with Y). Replacing the right side
by the local tensor algebra would delete the very collective observables
this exploration seeks. Conversely, merely adding an unrelated quantum
ancilla cannot count as an arithmetic survivor.

Distinguish an isolated scalar observable algebra from an object identified
with the tensor unit. Also distinguish a reference approaching a pure state,
a sector of vanishing reference weight, and a genuine collapse of the
observable system under its stated probe equivalence. POS's zero reference
alone does not establish collapse of its full matrix algebra.

Classical sums of scalar algebras remain classical. Coherent multiplicity
spaces may supply matrix blocks, but their off-diagonal maps and arithmetic
origin must be exhibited. Sums with forbidden intertwiners must retain that
restriction. A zero-weight sector is not assigned a normalized state at zero
without an explicit conditional or graded construction.

All of the above are prelimit requirements. At the endpoint each operation
may be retained, restricted to a domain, replaced by a correspondence, or
absent. Ordinary CP composition in an ambient quantum category does not by
itself prove that the distinguished arithmetic remnants are closed under it.

## 4. First bounded work package: a composite with surviving Frobenius

The immediate deliverable is one worked family of assembled arithmetic
experiments whose boundary has noncommutative observables and an observable
Frobenius CP action. The boundary need not admit another application of the
assembly operation that produced it.

### Step 1 — specify the finite family and the meaning of collapse

Write a small source grammar using existing symplectic/field objects,
retained arithmetic amplitudes, collective contexts, tensors, sums and code
maps. State the quantum realization of each operation. Name the interpolation
parameter t, its arithmetic comparison points, the reference functionals and
which data vary; no limit over a sequence of primes tending to one is meant.

Specify the probes on an isolated constituent and on a full composite.
Say whether the proposed isolated collapse concerns its algebra, its
restricted probes, or its zeroth-order reference. Start with degree-one
constituents and finite composite diagrams; do not impose a theorem that
every individually conditioned system must collapse.

### Step 2 — reproduce the composition benchmark without conflating it with arithmetic

Use the admitted Hecke levels n=1,2,3:

    H_1(1)=C, H_2(1)=C direct-sum C,
    H_3(1)=C direct-sum C direct-sum M_2(C).

Recover the existing standard-block experiment and its endpoint return
probability 1/4 (the initial return is 1). Retain its actual preparation
weight when it is prepared from the full reference. Compare independent
tensor, collective assembly, tagged sums, and source-permitted coherent
sums. Use n=4 only to check the first nontrivial reassociation paths.

This is a control example for the mechanism and the checker. Reproducing it
alone is not the new research result. Its local invisible exchange must not
be renamed Frobenius.

### Step 3 — transport actual Frobenius into a composite boundary

Start with F2 subset F4 and F3 subset F9. Retain field multiplication,
absolute and relative Frobenius, J/V transfers, Fourier, and one marked code
or context. Couple the existing positive arithmetic profiles to actual
composite tests. Keep all degrees of the finite profile before conditioning.

The first construction route is the algebra/operator system generated by
the actual joint context and arithmetic operators in those examples.
Extract boundary matrix elements or positive probe responses from that
joint system. Do not declare it to be a product of the Hecke and orbit
boundaries without proving the mixed relations and reference positivity.

If a global mixed family is unavailable, restrict to one explicit assembled
experiment and its finite required continuations. This is an acceptable
first result if its boundary system and Frobenius action are well defined.

Demand an endpoint observable distinction using a coherence-sensitive test,
in addition to any classical label permutation. Compare full Frobenius,
identity substitution, and deliberate dephasing. Identify a matrix block
or an explicitly reconstructed noncommutative quantum realization.

Only after a first witness works, add F2 subset F4 subset F16 for tower
coherence and a characteristic-dividing-degree case such as F3 subset F27.
For the tested branch, state every restriction; a general all-degree
theorem is a later extension unless the same proof already covers it.

### Step 4 — record what the boundary actually retains

For the successful finite family, provide an endpoint table of objects,
states, effects, Frobenius processes, and surviving comparisons. Test matrix
positivity and the retained CP maps, including required ancillas and full
instrument tags. State which sequential circuits still have arithmetic
lifts. Separately record the status of tensor, collective assembly, tagged
sum, coherent sum and corner operations: inherited / partial / absent.

Keep the full prelimit history as provenance when an operation is absent.
A CP envelope freely closed under more operations may be useful later,
but any such extension must be labelled as added structure.

### Step 5 — deliver and review one positive result

Deliver proposed definitions and scoped claims, one or two structured proof
shards, an independent exact checker with named mathematical mutations, a
worked example and a readable labbook section. Use the repository's capped
prove -> attack -> repair process only on the new positive statements.
The concrete first work order is
[composite-boundary-target.md](../../briefs/composite-boundary-target.md).

## 5. Acceptance and preregistered falsifiers

| Required evidence for the first new result | Data mutation that must expose loss |
|---|---|
| A collective observable absent from the isolated local description | Replace the composite algebra by the local tensor image |
| Honest distinction between tagged alternatives and coherent sums | Delete source-permitted off-diagonal blocks |
| A nonzero normalized quantum remnant with its actual preparation order | Drop all positive grades or reset a transported reference |
| Actual arithmetic Frobenius acts detectably on the remnant | Substitute identity for Frobenius |
| A coherence-sensitive operational distinction | Dephase the relevant register at the specified point |
| Mixed arithmetic provenance | Change a multiplication-table entry or a transfer/Fourier phase used by the witness |
| Every retained branch has its correct probability and target | Omit a failure tag, alter a branch normalization, or merge inequivalent histories |
| Claimed retained comparison diagrams agree | Change one prelimit assembly/embedding map along one path |

Preregister only gates the selected prototype actually uses. Endpoint
tensor/sum closure is deliberately NOT an acceptance gate. If an isolated
collapse is part of the headline statement, compute it with the declared
probe class; a pure-state reference does not pass an algebra-collapse gate.
All finite checker successes remain falsifier evidence, not universal proofs.

## 6. After the first witness: reconstruct Q_1 only as far as warranted

Describe the remnant through its admissible quantum probes, including
matrix-valued probes and context dependence. Investigate operator-system
or C*-algebra reconstruction from the limiting data. The functor-of-points
analogy guides this: a represented limit may exist even when a conventional
arithmetic representing object does not.

Test equality under the contexts that still exist at the boundary. Where
they are closed under wiring, attempt an operational quotient category.
Where they are not, retain a specified family of objects and processes with
partial comparisons. Do not infer a total operation from a remembered
prelimit expression. Tensor products of operator systems, if introduced,
require a named order/tensor construction and their own arithmetic comparison.

There are two separate directions after the prototype:

1. Enlarge the class of finite assembled families with a proved boundary.
2. Determine whether those boundaries possess additional composition laws.

Progress in the first direction does not depend on a positive answer in the
second. This is the user's latest clarification of the intended programme.

## 7. Frobenius implementation and the spectral direction

For each retained Frobenius record separately its CP map, any implementing
operator or distinguished dilation, the reference representation, and its
trace/moment functional. A bare unitary channel identifies implementations
that differ by scalar phase; spectral claims must state the extra datum or
canonical construction that fixes the intended implementation.

First compute the existing finite orbit trace/determinant identities with
their actual multiplicities and normalizations. Distinguish an ordinary
operator trace, a reference-state expectation and any graded trace. Ask
which of these identities survives the chosen boundary prescription.
Identify the arithmetic point functor being counted; do not exchange field
elements, embeddings and scheme-valued points without comparison maps.

Next choose and justify a completion across extension degrees or composite
sizes. A bounded finite register is not itself the proposed infinite
spectral object. Study the order of this completion and the p-to-one limit.
If nontrivial dynamics requires rescaled iterates, specify their time scale,
prove a limiting CP flow, and identify its generator. A generator is not
itself required to be CP. Such parameter-dependent analysis requires new
proofs beyond POS-FINITE.

The later acceptance target is an arithmetic trace or determinant relation
derived from the construction. A Riemann-zero comparison must arise through
that relation, with the relevant global assembly and completion specified;
fitting a spectrum to a list of zeros is not the construction sought here.

## 8. Decision rules and execution order

Proceed in order: finite specification -> composition control -> actual
Frobenius witness -> scoped boundary reconstruction -> tower/spectral work.
Do not start several alternative categorical programmes simultaneously.

If the first candidate collapses, use its failed probe to choose one forward
change: retain a higher grade or multiplicity space, enlarge the actual
composite observables, or weaken the required comparison. If every fixed
finite composite collapses under that prescription, consider a jointly
growing composite size/degree and t-to-one limit, with its scale stated.
That is a new branch, not a hidden change of the first finite theorem.

If only scalar probabilities survive, inspect matrix-valued probes and
arithmetic coherences before concluding that the boundary is classical.
If Frobenius disappears in an invariant commutant, retain the acted-on
register/controlled context instead of replacing Frobenius by an unrelated
unitary. If no positive construction emerges from a bounded attempt, record
the surviving narrower result and the concrete next route; do not extend
the hostile-review loop to search for a construction.

At each research increment allocate roughly half the effort to its one
construction, about a third to worked examples/checkers/labbook, and the
remainder to the single repair and integration. Independence of prover,
verifier and target-blind critic follows the existing repository protocol;
record their actual runtime/model metadata rather than importing stale
model assumptions from historical handoffs.

New mathematics enters definitions, claims and the owning labbook section
together after adjudication, with the appropriate green/red and PDF gates.
This planning turn does not resume FCR-2, admit the frozen marked-module
drafts, promote MIX-ALL or LIM-SIGNED, or begin an open-ended spectral search.
