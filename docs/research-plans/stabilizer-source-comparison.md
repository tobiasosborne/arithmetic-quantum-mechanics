# SP-STAB-REL source and interface audit

Date: 2026-09-10. Model: `gpt-5.6-sol`, reasoning setting `xhigh`.

Coordinator landing note, same date: this preserves the pre-proof source
audit. The subsequent constructive SP-STAB-REL proof discharges our bare
dagger and equivalence clauses; see the canonical claim and
`../../theory/verdicts/phantasm-relations-adjudication.md`. The source
statements and convention differences below retain their recorded scope.

This audit uses the canonical D1701--D1706 definitions and SP-STAB-REL
contract, and the locally hashed primary bodies SP-CK21, SP-BC24 and
SP-GROSS06. It does not read either relation proof/checker lane. Labels below
distinguish `[SOURCE]` statements actually made in a source, `[CALC]`
elementary convention conversions carried out here, and `[OPEN]` comparison
lemmas still required. Nothing here proves or promotes SP-STAB-REL.

The principal finding is precise. SP-CK21 Theorem `theorem:spekkens` states a
**symmetric monoidal equivalence**, not a dagger equivalence. SP-BC24 supplies
an explicit dagger-compact presentation of affine Lagrangian relations, but
its dagger is time-reversed converse rather than D1702's bare relational
converse. This does not refute the canonical claim: the bare-converse map may
still match Hilbert adjoint under the required generator dictionary. It means
that dagger preservation is a separate local lemma, not a clause imported
from the cited theorem.

## What the sources actually establish

### SP-CK21

- `[SOURCE]` Objects have standard coordinates `[X|Z]`; subspaces are row
  spaces and the form matrix is `[[0,I],[-I,0]]` (`lagrel.tex` 1722--1757).
- `[SOURCE]` `LagRel_k` has morphisms described as Lagrangian subspaces,
  relational composition and direct-sum tensor (`lagrel.tex` 1760--1762).
  Tensoring two symplectic objects regroups the blocks by
  `1 + swap + 1`; the forgetful functor to linear relations is faithful
  strong symmetric monoidal (`lagrel.tex` 1807--1820).
- `[SOURCE]` The paper says the linear category is compact closed and uses a
  cap to curry every process into a state (`lagrel.tex` 1860--1940).
- `[SOURCE]` Possibly empty affine relations are explicit in `AffRel_k`
  (`lagrel.tex` 2921--2929). `AffLagRel_k` is then defined as generated by
  linear Lagrangian relations and all affine shifts; its forgetful functor is
  faithful but only strong monoidal (`lagrel.tex` 3096--3123).
- `[SOURCE]` The named symplectic generators are row/right-action matrices:
  `F=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`, `S_a=[[1,a],[0,1]]`, and the displayed four-block
  `C_a`; their column effects are also stated in words (`lagrel.tex`
  2299--2419).
- `[SOURCE]` The quantum gates are
  `X|j>=|j+1>`, `C|j,k>=|j,j+k>`, positive-kernel
  `F=p^(-1/2)(zeta^(jk))`, and
  `S|j>=exp(pi i j(j+p)/p)|j>` (`lagrel.tex` 3585--3643).
- `[SOURCE]` `Stab_p` is the subcategory of complex matrices generated by the
  qudit Clifford group and `|0>`, `<0|`, quotiented by invertible scalars
  (`lagrel.tex` 3647--3649). The state comparison maps the empty relation to
  zero and maps the displayed state/affine/linear generators to
  `|0>`, `X`, `C`, `F`, `S` (`lagrel.tex` 3652--3701).
- `[SOURCE]` Theorem `theorem:spekkens` extends the state comparison by
  currying and the `Z`-observable cap and calls the result a **symmetric
  monoidal equivalence** (`lagrel.tex` 3703--3744). The theorem statement does
  not say dagger, dagger symmetric monoidal, or dagger compact.

### SP-BC24

- `[SOURCE]` The skeletal standard object uses interleaved slots
  `(z_0,x_0,...,z_(n-1),x_(n-1))` and
  `omega((z,x),(z',x'))=z*x'-x*z'` (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 21--39).
- `[SOURCE]` A relation `m->n` is an affine Lagrangian subspace for
  `omega_m(input)-omega_n(output)`; composition is ordinary relational
  composition, identity is diagonal, tensor is direct sum with unit zero
  (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 143--179). The ambient form is the global negative
  of D1702's `-omega_input+omega_output`, so it defines exactly the same
  Lagrangian subsets.
- `[SOURCE]` Empty affine subspaces count as isotropic/coisotropic/Lagrangian
  (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 105--118), and nontransverse state/effect
  composition can be empty (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 191--205).
- `[SOURCE]` Its compact cup/cap is the graph of the anti-symplectic
  involution `c(z,x)=(z,-x)`, namely `(z,x,z,-x)`
  (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 162--167).
- `[SOURCE]` Its dagger is
  `R -> (c_output + c_input)(R^converse)`, not bare converse
  (`section_AffLagRel.tex` 169--177).
- `[SOURCE]` The explicit spider semantics and presentation are dagger
  symmetric monoidal; the final interpretation is a dagger-compact prop
  isomorphism with affine Lagrangian relations (`section_AffLagRel.tex`
  263--345 and 389--402, 568--579).
- `[SOURCE]` The quantum application says that the paper's dagger corresponds
  to Hilbert adjoint and describes the stabilizer comparison as a
  dagger-compact prop isomorphism modulo scalars, while attributing it to
  SP-CK21 (`section_Applications.tex` 140--174). This is a later summary, not
  the statement of SP-CK21 `theorem:spekkens`.

### SP-GROSS06

- `[SOURCE]` For odd `d`, Gross uses momentum/position coordinates `(p,q)`,
  `w(p,q)=zeta^(-p*q/2) Z(p)X(q)`, the same standard symplectic matrix, and
  tensor products in grouped order `(p_1,...,p_n,q_1,...,q_n)`
  (`poswig.tex` 340--420).
- `[SOURCE]` A Clifford unitary maps Weyl operators to scalar multiples of
  Weyl operators (`poswig.tex` 458--466). The Clifford structure theorem says
  symplectic maps have projective unitary implementers and every Clifford is,
  up to phase, `w(r) mu(S)` (`poswig.tex` 477--537).
- `[SOURCE]` This is unitary/phase-space scope. It does not construct the
  category generated by preparations and effects, its zero arrows, its
  complex-scalar quotient, or a dagger equivalence of relation categories.

## Exact coordinate and sign dictionary

| interface | exact conversion | status |
|---|---|---|
| D1703 standard object to CK21 | D's grouped column `(a;b)` has the same algebraic form matrix as CK21's grouped row `[X|Z]`. To compare a CK row/right-action matrix with a D column action, transpose it. CK's slot names should not be silently identified with BC24's later `Z,X` names. | `[SOURCE]+[CALC]` |
| Tensor order | CK21 changes `(X_1,Z_1,X_2,Z_2)` to `(X_1,X_2;Z_1,Z_2)` by `1+swap+1`. D1702 makes this same regrouping explicit, and D1703 uses `(a_1,a_2;b_1,b_2)`. BC24 begins interleaved and therefore needs the analogous fixed permutation to D's grouped order. | `[SOURCE]` |
| Relation input sign | BC24's `omega_in-omega_out` is `-1` times D1702's `-omega_in+omega_out`; multiplying a form by `-1` does not change orthogonals or Lagrangian subspaces. Thus the identity-on-underlying-relations matches these two signed Hom-sets, composition and identity. | `[CALC]` |
| CK21 positive-form prose | Read literally, CK21 1761 places a morphism in the positive standard form after grouping, while 1750 displays `Gamma_f={(f v,v)}`. A one-sided anti-symplectic correction maps the forms but sends the diagonal identity to `graph(c)` and inserts `c` on a composed middle leg, so it is not a functor with ordinary relational composition. BC24 143--189 is the safe explicit signed formulation. | `[SOURCE]+[CALC]` |
| D1703 to Gross | `p=-b`, `q=a`. Then Gross's `w(p,q)` is exactly D1703's `W^s(a,b)`, including phase, and Gross's symplectic form becomes `a*b'-a'*b`. | `[CALC]` |
| Empty/zero | D1702 empty relation maps to the zero linear map class. C-times never carries zero to nonzero, so zero is a separate quotient orbit. CK21 also explicitly uses the uniqueness of the empty relation and zero matrix in every type. | `[SOURCE]+[CALC]` |

The proposed input-only transport `R -> {(c v,w):(v,w) in R}` is therefore
rejected as a category bridge: even where it fixes the form sign, it fails
the diagonal identity and ordinary composition. No such transport should
appear in a proof without changing the target composition law.

## Generator dictionary to D1703--D1705

The following table records actual matrices rather than relying on colour
names. Let a CK21 symplectic generator act on a row by `r -> r M`. Combining
that right action with the displayed source graph order `(M r,r)` gives the D
column action

    g_D = (M^(-1))^T.                                      (C1)

`(C1)` is an elementary conversion, not part of the source theorem. It must be
stated when integrating the source presentation; otherwise the Fourier and
shear signs appear to disagree.

| source generator | source quantum map | D-side actual map/operator | adjoint required by D1705 |
|---|---|---|---|
| affine shift | `X|j>=|j+1>` | `X(1)=W^s(1,0)`, hence affine translation `t=(1,0)` under D1703/SP-EGOROV | `X(-1)`, translation `-t` |
| `F` | positive kernel `p^(-1/2) zeta^(jk)` | From (C1), `J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`; direct D1703 calculation gives `F W^s(a,b) F^*=W^s(b,-a)` | negative-kernel `F^*=F^(-1)`, graph `J^(-1)` |
| `S_a` | at `a=1`, `diag exp(pi i j(j+p)/p)=diag zeta^(j^2/2)` | From (C1), `(a_D,b_D)->(a_D,b_D-a*a_D)`; this is the D1703 lower shear with parameter `-a` | replace `a` by `-a`; graph inverse |
| `C_a` | at `a=1`, `|j,k>->|j,j+k>` | From (C1), `(a_1,a_2;b_1,b_2)->(a_1,a*a_1+a_2;b_1-a*b_2,b_2)` | controlled subtraction; graph inverse |
| zero state/effect | source maps the displayed state to `|0>` and includes `<0|` | D1704's `1->delta_0` and its adjoint | exchange ket and bra |
| empty | zero matrix | D1705's separate zero class | zero |

The linear entries can be checked directly from CK21's displayed matrices
at 2320--2394. The D-side Fourier/shear identities also follow directly from
D1703's wavefunction formula. The affine diagram itself is graphical in
CK21, so `[OPEN]` the integration proof must evaluate its relation semantics
and show that its sign agrees with the table; mapping it to `X` by name alone
is insufficient.

For the dagger, D1702 gives bare converse, so the graph of `g` goes to the
graph of `g^(-1)`. The last column shows that the target maps then go to their
Hilbert adjoints. This is the positive generator-by-generator route to the
canonical dagger clause. It still must be written for a generating set and
extended through composition/tensor; SP-CK21's theorem does not do so in its
statement.

## The two dagger conventions are genuinely distinct

Let `c(z,x)=(z,-x)` and take the upper shear
`g_a=[[1,a],[0,1]]`, with `a != 0` in odd characteristic.

- `[CALC]` D1702 bare converse sends `graph(g_a)` to
  `graph(g_a^(-1))=graph([[1,-a],[0,1]])`.
- `[CALC]` BC24 dagger sends it to `graph(c g_a^(-1) c)=graph(g_a)`.

The relations differ. The difference is not repaired by the fact that both
are valid dagger structures. The compact structures also differ in their
typing: the planned canonical construction uses an opposite-form dual and a
diagonal cup, while BC24 identifies the standard object with its dual using
`c` and obtains the self-dual cup `(z,x,z,-x)`. Applying `c` on the dual factor
does turn the opposite-form diagonal into the BC cup; that is a useful compact
comparison, but it does not by itself prove the bare-converse/Hilbert-adjoint
claim for arbitrary morphisms.

## Exact strength available for SP-STAB-REL

What may be reused from SP-CK21 is an odd-prime, all-arities, modulo-C-times
**symmetric monoidal equivalence** between its affine Lagrangian prop and its
generated stabilizer prop, with the displayed object and generator map.
SP-GROSS06 supports the Clifford/affine-symplectic unitary part. SP-BC24 gives
a cleaner signed definition of affine Lagrangian relations and an exact
dagger-compact graphical presentation, but uses its explicit time-reversal
dagger.

The following comparisons remain, in dependency order:

1. `[OPEN: relation convention]` Identify D1702's standard signed relation
   prop with the source prop, including the row/column graph direction,
   affine phase-one sign, tensor regrouping, diagonal identity and empty
   relation. Use BC24 for the signed Hom-set/category convention and CK21 for
   the stabilizer theorem; do not use the failed input-only `c` transport.
2. `[OPEN: generator map]` Apply (C1) and the affine calculation to every
   source generator. Verify against D1703's `Z(-b)X(a)` convention, including
   positive Fourier and quadratic-phase signs.
3. `[OPEN: target identification]` Prove the CK Pauli normalizer and D1307
   `C_2(A)` agree projectively; then prove that D1704's freely adjoined actual
   complex scalars disappear exactly under D1705, with zero still separate.
4. `[OPEN: theorem transport]` Conjugate SP-CK21's symmetric monoidal
   equivalence through items 1--3. This supplies fullness, faithfulness and
   essential surjectivity at the exact D1702/D1705 types.
