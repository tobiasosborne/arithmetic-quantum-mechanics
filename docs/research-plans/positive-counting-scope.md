# Scope of the positive counting construction

Goal campaign, 2026-09-09. This was the acceptance/scope specification.
The POS theorems are now admitted; their precise result is recorded in
positive-counting-result.md and the proof adjudication. The user clarified
that finding what remains is part of the project; no intrinsic
characteristic-independent endpoint is assumed.

The candidate is a counting/reference specialization of complete arithmetic
experiments. It fixes an actual prime p and actual finite-field diagram,
including every field table and named phase. The formal counting parameter
is t=exp(h), with the arithmetic comparison point t=p and boundary t=1.
The underlying amplitude/CP maps remain their actual matrices. Additional
reference preparations vary with h according to the uniquely specified
period-uniform masses. They need not be tracial or invariant under Fourier.

The positive content to earn is more than a constant operator envelope:

1. One canonical positive state prescription on every full field register,
   natural for all named field embeddings and their code restrictions,
   with exact arithmetic comparison and the prescribed Frobenius counts.
2. A common positive rule for ALL mixed moments/circuits from these inputs;
   actual coherent arithmetic relations, singular degrees and retained
   measurement histories remain intact. No intermediate sector compression.
3. A finite positive boundary profile sufficient for every leading event
   coefficient and conditional output under all fixed arithmetic CP
   continuations, including later postselection and entangling tensor.
4. A uniform mixed arithmetic protocol detecting relative Frobenius,
   multiplication, Fourier and transfer in every proper finite extension.
   It must exhibit both arithmetic distinction and measurable coherence.

Candidate universal content includes degree-dependent Frobenius moments,
the moving-orbit matrix systems, code-failure leading weights, interaction
orders for independently prepared nonzero controls, and the uniform 0/1
mixed-protocol outcome. These must be demonstrated, not inferred from
the continued existence of finite matrices.

Data that may remain include p, field cardinality, field multiplication
tables, cyclotomic phases, and multiplicity spaces. Full nonzero-endpoint
purity and Fourier probabilities can still distinguish p=2 from p=3.
Do not call the resulting whole theory intrinsic over characteristic one.

The finite profile discards higher Taylor data as independent state data;
the theorem must prove that those data are unnecessary for the stated
leading operational questions. Exact finite-h probabilities and arbitrary
h-dependent cancellation/angle operations are outside that adequacy claim.
Leading conditional states alone are not closed under later rare-event
conditioning: retain the full profile before composing or conditioning.

The reference is tied to the arithmetic field structure. A coordinate
unitary from H_E to prime-field sites transports its state; it does not
reset it to the product reference on independent sites. Likewise copying
one rare register is not independent preparation of two rare registers.
For x=y!=0 the independent event has order two in this prescription, while
the copied event has order one. The old ordinary polynomial point count
for that coupled event is not the new positive mixed-counting rule.

The previous MIX-CHART/TOWER construction varies angle operators while
retaining logical arithmetic factors. Its exact identification with this
state-counting specialization is not supplied by fixed operator relations.
MIX-ALL, which asks for that stronger combined realization, must not be
promoted on the strength of this candidate alone.

The clarified minimum goal can be assessed only after the above positive
results survive independent proof/verification review and the remaining
dependence is reported plainly. Neither a false tracial continuation nor
an arbitrary auxiliary matrix block qualifies as a solution.
