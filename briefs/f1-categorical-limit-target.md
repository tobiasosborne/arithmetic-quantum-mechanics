## Objective and scope

Construct a fully specified category of quantum subsystem families and
composable processes over the positive type-A Hecke family, with genuine
finite C*-algebraic states, effects, instruments, context dependence,
assembly, and evaluation at q=1. Arithmetic fibres have q=Q=p^r.
Prove the finite-experiment limit and endpoint lifting statements, including
the hypotheses needed for postselection. Compare the category with its
universal algebraic description, physical exchange, Schur-Weyl realizations,
fusion quotients, and vector/phase/polarization extensions.

The endpoint must contain the existing collective qubit and contextual
process witnesses. A sequence of primes approaching one is not involved.
No identification of flag contexts with the entire Weyl system may be
smuggled into the definition. Every broader comparison must specify its
actual functor, correspondence, quotient, or extra datum.

- Explicit objects, morphisms, equality, identity, composition, tensor or
  ordered assembly, adjoints where appropriate, and all coherence maps.
- A stated operational realization with positive normalized states, CP
  instruments, Born rule, preparation, marginalization and retained contexts.
- Evaluation functors at q>0 and at one, respecting the declared structure.
- A proved finite-protocol continuity theorem; conditioning requires a
  positive limiting event probability.
- Endpoint state/effect/instrument/process lifting with the actual interval
  or germ hypotheses, not an unsupported claim of a global canonical lift.
- A worked three-constituent experiment including a refinement and ancillary
  context, and an explicit composite-state example.
- All headline mathematical statements promoted only after independent
  critique and repair, with no discrepancy in exact falsifiers.
