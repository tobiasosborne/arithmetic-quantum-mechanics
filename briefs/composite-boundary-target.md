## Objective

Construct one explicit finite family of arithmetic composite experiments
with a positive p-to-one boundary, noncommutative quantum content, and an
actual arithmetic Frobenius represented by an observable CP process.

The prelimit family must support independent tensors, collective assembly,
tagged sums and source-permitted coherent additive constructions. Tensor
and sum operations may BOTH fail to descend to the boundary. A conventional
C_1 and monoidal closure of the boundary Q_1 are not required.

The first witness may be restricted to named finite diagrams. State its
precise scope and the retained characteristic data. A general theorem is
desirable where the proof permits it; finite checks do not extend its scope.

## Bounded starting point

1. Use the already admitted Hecke levels one through three as a composition
   control. Reproduce the level-three matrix block and its 1/4 return
   experiment. Level four checks reassociation. This is existing evidence,
   not the new result and not a Frobenius construction.
2. Start actual arithmetic with F2 subset F4 and F3 subset F9, using FRB/FRP
   generators and POS references/profiles. Retain one marked code/context,
   actual Frobenius, multiplication and the J/V/Fourier comparisons used by
   the selected circuit. Build joint observables from their actual matrices.
3. Seek a positive boundary for one already assembled composite experiment.
   Retain all needed reference grades, source-permitted off-diagonal maps,
   coherent amplitudes and measurement histories. A bare tensor product of
   existing endpoint algebras is only a proposal until its arithmetic
   comparison and mixed reference are demonstrated.
4. After obtaining a candidate, test F2 subset F4 subset F16 and, where
   relevant, F3 subset F27. Do not infer invertible-degree formulas in a
   characteristic-dividing degree.

## Construction questions to answer in the first draft

- What are the arithmetic objects, morphisms and assembly operations?
- Which CP maps are supplied by source amplitudes/context data, and what
  extra maps, if any, are deliberately added as an operational envelope?
- What is the common parameter and comparison with actual arithmetic?
- Which isolated probes become trivial, if that collapse is claimed?
  Distinguish pure reference limits from a collapse of observables.
- What is the complete composite observable system before specialization?
  Exhibit its collective or off-diagonal operators and their provenance.
- What boundary space and matrix-positive structure actually exist?
- Which state/effect experiment detects the endpoint Frobenius, and which
  additional test distinguishes preserved coherence from dephasing?
- Which compositions have endpoint comparisons, and which cease to exist
  as operations on the selected remnant? Give the domains of partial ones.
- Which Frobenius lift, reference representation and trace survive for
  later spectral work? Do not identify channel and implementer spectra.

## Independent checker specification

Preregister exact arithmetic at the small comparison fibres. Use rational
or cyclotomic arithmetic where possible. If a genuinely varying matrix
family needs numerical evaluation, state its norm/tolerance and an
independent exact invariant. Its all-parameter positivity needs a proof.

At minimum, the chosen prototype's gates must detect:

- discarding a collective observable by replacing assembly with local tensor;
- deleting required coherent off-diagonal blocks or confusing them with tags;
- truncating a needed reference grade;
- replacing the actual Frobenius by identity;
- dephasing the coherence used in the witness;
- altering a used multiplication entry or Fourier/transfer datum;
- losing an instrument outcome or using the wrong branch normalization;
- breaking a comparison diagram that the construction actually claims.

Each advertised red mode must fail at a named mathematical gate, rather
than through an interpreter error or an unconditional failure switch.
Do not test endpoint tensor or direct-sum closure as a required property.
Freeze the finite scope and retain actual output records.

## Deliverables and admission

Before proof: a finite source/realization specification and preregistered
checker expectations, including the exact boundary nontriviality witness.

After construction: proposed definitions/claims and notation changes,
structured derivations in one or two bounded shards, the independent
checker/results, and a self-contained worked labbook example. Proposals
remain outside the authoritative registries until the capped review.

Run one target-blind hostile round on the new positive statement, one repair
wave, and mechanical adjudication. Apply the resulting honest scope; do not
re-review already admitted controls or promote unrelated conjectures.

Admission requires a positive quantum remnant and detectable arithmetic
Frobenius from the stated composite construction. Classical label data
alone, an unrelated appended matrix block, or a surviving formal Frobenius
symbol do not supply that result.

If the finite attempt fails, give its precise failing probe and one
constructive next adjustment (higher grades, actual multiplicity/context
data, weaker comparisons, or a declared growing-composite limit). Record
any proved narrower construction without claiming the target achieved.

The general matrix-probe reconstruction and the infinite-degree spectral
programme follow this work package. They are not prerequisites for its
finite result and do not extend its review budget.
