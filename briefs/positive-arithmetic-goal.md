# Positive arithmetic counting boundary — goal campaign, 2026-09-09

The user explicitly requested an active goal: a rigorous arithmetic theory
overcoming the established blockers and permitting a nontrivial useful
p-to-one limit. The goal is active. The user clarified that the bare minimum
is that something nontrivial remains, and determining what remains is itself
part of the project. No characteristic-independent endpoint is imposed in
advance. The theory must identify universal content and retained arithmetic
labels explicitly. A constant matrix envelope alone is insufficient: a
claimed solution needs a positive common counting rule, coherent mixed
protocols and a substantive operational boundary theorem.

This campaign uses the standard capped proof -> hostile review -> one repair
wave -> mechanical adjudication. Preserve all-characteristic, all-degree and
all-finite-diagram scopes where asserted. Small fields are falsifiers only.
About half the effort is construction, a third exact tests/labbook, and at
most a fifth repairs/hygiene. Existing arithmetic proofs are not reopened
except where a new claim actually depends on them.

## Candidate construction to prove or reject

Fix an actual characteristic p and a finite named field diagram. Let
E=F_(p^r), with the full arithmetic register H_E=ell^2(E) and all the
admitted actual amplitude/CP maps. Distinguish the interpolation variable
t>=1 from the retained characteristic p. Put h=log t.

Partition E into {0} and its nonzero exact Frobenius-period-d labels, d|r.
Let P_(E,0)=|0><0| and P_(E,d)^times be the corresponding projections.
Their cardinalities are b_1(p)=p-1 and b_d(p)=c_d(p) for d>1, where c_d
is D1331's Moebius polynomial. Put b_1(t)=t-1, b_d(t)=c_d(t) for d>1.
The proposed unnormalized density and normalized reference are

    D_E(h)=P_(E,0)+sum_(d|r) b_d(exp h)/b_d(p) P_(E,d)^times,
    rho_E(h)=exp(-r h) D_E(h).

Targets to establish:

1. Positivity, trace D_E(h)=exp(r h), faithful rho for h>0, and the exact
   arithmetic comparison rho_E(log p)=I/p^r. At h=0 the reference is the
   zero ket. The interpolation is generally a STATE, not a tracial state.
   Prove uniqueness under the explicit class-uniformity and prescribed
   period-mass conditions; name all retained arithmetic/phase choices.
2. Positive coefficients: D_E(h)=sum_(k>=0) h^k A_(E,k), with A_0=P0 and
   A_k=sum_(d|r) J_k(d)/(k! b_d(p)) P_d for k>=1, including J_k(1)=1.
   Here J_k(d)=d^k product_(ell|d prime)(1-ell^-k). Each A_k is positive;
   trace A_k=r^k/k!. For k>=1 it is faithful on the nonzero-label subspace.
   Hence rho_E is an exact Poisson mixture of the normalized A_k states.
3. For every named embedding i:K->E, absolute Frobenius periods and zero
   are preserved, and J_i^*D_E(h)J_i=D_K(h). Thus canonical code success
   probability is t^(s-r), and conditioning returns rho_K. Prove all named
   towers, including p dividing the degrees. No division by n is used.
4. Reference Frobenius moments satisfy Tr(rho_E(h) U_E^k)=
   t^(gcd(r,k)-r). On independently prepared words, multiplication active
   mass is ((t^r-1)/t^r)^d, with the earlier exact gate-trace and squared
   difference formulas. Conditioning on moving Frobenius labels recovers
   the admitted FRL conditional weights on its selected orbit algebra.
5. Keep ALL actual amplitude maps and their coherent compositions on the
   full register. Their interpretations are constant in h and retain every
   source equation. Do not compress each intermediate gate to a sector.
   Actual CP maps act coefficientwise on the positive operator series.
   Tensor is the Cauchy product of coefficients; ordinary trace, not the
   new reference functional, tests channel normalization.
6. For any fixed finite CP branch Phi and finite tensor reference, prove
   its success series has nonnegative coefficients and its first nonzero
   coefficient gives a positive normalized conditional endpoint. Decide
   what category of positive series / graded quantum ensembles / reference
   experiments makes composition, equality and evaluation precise. A list
   of coefficient states is not automatically a functor on conditioned

7. Fourier carries D_E to its actual conjugate F_E D_E F_E^*. Treat those
   as different reference contexts. The J/V Fourier square should give
   coherent transported reference/code instruments, including singular

   Never infer invariance from the arithmetic point h=log p.
8. Establish a genuine mixed arithmetic witness: full relative Frobenius,
   Fourier, multiplication and transfer must be detected in one explicitly

## What this candidate does not get for free

- The matrix representation of the arithmetic source is constant in h.
  This is not by itself a varying-characteristic category or a proof of an
  intrinsic characteristic-independent F1 theory. Its possible value is the
  canonical nontracial counting reference, positive graded conditioning and
  global coherent mixed-moment rule, with explicit retained labels.
- The canonical maximally mixed preparation remains its actual constant
  arithmetic map. The new rho_E(h) is additional reference data, coinciding
  with it only at the arithmetic comparison point. Do not preserve a false
  Fourier-invariance equation for this new reference.
- The prior MIX-CHART/TOWER continuation changes actual angle matrices.
  This candidate initially changes states only. Its compatibility with those
  prior chosen continuations is a theorem to prove or an honest distinction.
- A Taylor-series/graded construction must state what is retained and what
  information is discarded. Keeping every coefficient is not automatically
  an informative limit; show finite-order operational utility and exact
  composition/conditioning properties.
- No theorem is allowed to silently replace the old multiplication gate
  on field values by multiplication of a Galois group or an idempotent basis.

## Preregistered falsifiers

P1. Independent finite-field census and exact density coefficients for prime
and extension fields, including p=2,3,5 and degrees through four where cheap.
Check normalization, comparison at t=p, nonzero/moving cuts and moments.
P2. Actual named inclusions and a Frobenius-twisted embedding; exact all-zero
and nonzero sector restrictions, towers with degrees divisible by p, and
Fourier-transported code reference identities. Reject naive Fourier invariance.
P3. A generic joint-support projection formerly assigned negative weight
must now have nonnegative expectation under the constructed STATE. Exhibit
the difference from the old tracial continuation numerically and analytically.
P4. Full multiplication circuit with a path leaving/returning to a sector,
including coherent input; compare full evolution, actual measured histories,
and forbidden intermediate compression separately. Actual phases required.
P5. Positive coefficient action under sequential CP maps, independent tensor,
postselection and a zero-leading success branch. Test the first nonzero grade
and normalized limiting state against exact direct series coefficients.
P6. Active multiplication arity d versus order d, and relative Frobenius
outside the old joint code space. Detect changed field multiplication data.
P7. Boundary dependence on retained p: compare endpoints of the same degree
at p=2 and p=3 and list which observables agree and which do not. This is a
scope test, not a request to hide dependence.
