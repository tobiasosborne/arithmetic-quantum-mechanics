## Gates and independently constructed data

- **G1:** Enumerate S3 permutations, its order-two subgroup, left cosets,
  regular action, quotient map and intersection of all conjugate subgroups.
  Check normal core trivial, faithful three-point action, equivariant
  fibre pullback, and ordinary-trace decoder reference success 1/2.
  Enumerate cyclic quotient maps and divisor towers through order 12.
- **G2:** Compare actual incidence matrices for ternary towers and Cartesian
  products. Evaluate success/failure CP branches on matrix units, retaining
  ss/sf/fs/ff independently. Check a coherent superposition across two
  simultaneous-cycle orbits distinguishes full quantum multiplicity from
  dephasing. Product success probabilities must be products.
- **G3:** Give a nonuniform finite-map tower whose individually normalized
  fibre pullbacks are isometries but do not compose. The constant-rank
  restriction must exclude this example.
- **F1:** Construct finite fields by polynomial arithmetic and determine
  embeddings and relative traces by exponentiation. Build J and unnormalized
  trace-fibre incidence B directly. Compare J*B, its squared Gram spectrum,
  support ranks and projection relations in F2->F4, F2->F8, F3->F9,
  F4->F16 and F3->F27. Expect the n-invertible scalar-permutation Gram,
  versus rank one with squared nonzero singular value q/kappa when p|n.
  The characteristic-two quadratic branch has an actual intersection.
- **F2:** Build negative-kernel character matrices from absolute traces,
  using exact second/third roots of unity and squared scalar normalizations.
  Check both Fourier-transfer identities, Frobenius intertwining, the
  invertible-degree logical D_n correction and both decoder outcomes.
  No arbitrary-characteristic Fourier test is claimed.
- **R1:** Recompute generic two-projection support weight 2/kappa and
  complementary weight 1-2/kappa at rational kappa near one. Negative
  complement values reject unconditioned positive continuation and motivate
  conditioning; they do not reject the conditional construction.
- **R2:** Independently evaluate the supplied conditional 2x2 construction
  using rational Pythagorean angles, its normalized off-diagonal direction,
  Fourier interchange, noncommutativity, a Born witness, retained CP
  instrument completeness, continuity at the specified boundary and all
  independent tensor outcomes. This gate remains pending until the proposed
  matrices and trace are supplied; no formula is silently invented as evidence.
- **M1:** Construct F4 multiplication and preserve the moving-sector leakage
  alpha,alpha,alpha^2 -> alpha,alpha,0. Compare a return circuit with an
  intermediate sector compression to the full circuit, and retain the
  measured success/failure histories. Also retain the known nonzero-cut V
  incompatibility and Fourier/moving-cut overlap.

## Red-first contract

Every advertised mutation changes mathematical data, never the expected
verdict or an unconditional assertion. Planned mutation families: coset-map
label, pullback normalization, missing tensor outcome, erased orbit
coherence, nonuniform composition treated as uniform, trace fibre label,
wrong Gram branch, omitted logical degree scaling, Fourier sign/scaling,
wrong failure projector, deleted physical support rank, missing tangent
normalization, collapsed tangent algebra, tangent Fourier, incomplete tangent
instrument, and compressed mixed transition. Each must fail its named gate
with a mathematical assertion, not an interpreter exception.

Run at least one red before any green; freeze every red and the final green
with command, exit status, gate, exact diagnostics, checker hash and bounded
sample inventory. Standard library plus numpy only. Rational matrices and
cyclotomic coefficients are exact; no numerical tolerance.
