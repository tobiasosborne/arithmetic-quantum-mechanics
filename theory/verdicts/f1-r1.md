## Numbered objections

### O1 — MAJOR: `CycCorr_N` is not coordinate-free on its stated objects

**(a) Location.** `theory/lanes/f1/prove/PATCH.md`, D1007, lines 95--104,
especially the clauses “matrices ... between their orbit sets” and
`Real_iota`; and `theory/lanes/f1/prove/f1-cyclotomic-kernel.md`, section 7
preamble, lines 378--384.

**(b) Independent computation.** For a free `mu_N`-orbit `o` in a pointed
set `S`, `C_iota(S)` contains the associated line
`L_o`, but the orbit supplies no preferred vector of that line.  Choose
representatives `s` of a source orbit and `t` of a target orbit and write a
linear map as `e_s |-> m e_t`.  Replacing them by `v s` and `u t` changes the
coordinate to

    m' = iota(v) iota(u)^(-1) m.

Thus a bare `K_N^cyc`-matrix indexed only by orbit sets does not canonically
define a map `C_iota(S)->C_iota(T)`.  With two or more orbits, independent
changes of representatives change different rows and columns, so this is not
removed by the later quotient by one global scalar.  The phrase
“mu_N-weighted correspondence” could repair this if it means an intrinsic
equivariant kernel or a torsor of maps between associated lines, but D1007
does not define such data and simultaneously identifies it with an ordinary
matrix.

**FIX DEMAND.** Either restrict objects to framed torsors `mu_N x I` with the
frame included in the datum, or define morphisms intrinsically as line-valued
or equivariant kernels and prove that composition, tensor, and `Real_iota`
are independent of representatives.

**SURVIVING WEAKER STATEMENT.** On the standard framed objects
`S_A={0} disjoint-union (mu_N x A)`, the section `a |-> [1,a]` makes the
displayed cyclotomic matrices and the Fourier matrix well-defined.

### O2 — MAJOR: the strong-monoidal conjecture has no monoidal source as defined

**(a) Location.** `theory/lanes/f1/prove/PATCH.md`, D1007, lines 106--112;
`theory/lanes/f1/prove/f1-cyclotomic-kernel.md`, F1-CORR statement and
`<1>4`, lines 397--418.

**(b) Independent computation.** D1007 gives objects, morphisms, and
composition for `LiftHyp_N`, but no tensor product on objects or morphisms, no
unit, and no associativity or symmetry constraints.  Consequently “strong
symmetric monoidal functor” is not yet a typed assertion.  The intended
repair is available: under
`V_(A x B) ~= V_A x V_B`, define

    (g,r) tensor (h,s) = (g x h, (v,w) |-> r(v)s(w)).

Because both ordered cocycles factor over direct products, multiplying the
two lift equations proves the lift equation for this tensor; `A=0` is the
unit.  These data and their coherence are absent from the definition.

The intended representation tensor is also safe only in the external,
central-product sense.  If `rho_A` and `rho_B` both have central character
`iota`, then on `rho_A tensor rho_B` the anti-diagonal element
`((u,1),(u^(-1),1))` acts by
`iota(u)iota(u^(-1))=1`; hence the tensor descends to
`H_N(A) boxdot H_N(B)`, whose remaining centre acts by `iota`.  This does not
make the fixed-central-character sector of `Rep(H_N(A))` internally tensor
closed, and the artifact does not claim that it does.

**FIX DEMAND.** Define the symmetric monoidal structures on `LiftHyp_N` and
`P(CycCorr_N)` explicitly, including tensor on lift cochains and the unit and
coherence maps, before retaining “strong symmetric monoidal.”

**SURVIVING WEAKER STATEMENT.** `LiftHyp_N` is a well-defined groupoid under
the stated composition, and the proposed objectwise tensor and external
central-product representation calculation are correct.

## Independently verified correct — repair boundary

The following survived independent recomputation and should not be churned in
the repair wave.

- **Character duality.** The extension argument is valid for every `N>=1`
  with `exp(A)|N`: if `m` is the order of `a+B`, then the required value lies
  in `mu_(d/m) <= mu_(N/m)`, the image of the `m`-th power map on `mu_N`.
  Separation, cardinality, and orthogonality follow.  This includes even `N`.

- **Heisenberg law and centre.** Direct composition gives
  `W(a,chi)W(b,eta)=eta(a)^(-1)W(a+b,chi eta)`.  Its alternating commutator is
  `chi(b)eta(a)^(-1)`, with trivial radical by character separation, so the
  centre is exactly the named `mu_N`.  For `A=C_2`, the four matrices are
  `I,X,Z,ZX`, with `(ZX)^2=-I`; for `A=C_3`, the same calculation gives the
  expected `zeta_3` cocycle.  No division by two occurs.

- **Matrix algebra and finite Stone--von Neumann.** The trace is zero for a
  nonzero translation and is the character sum for a diagonal operator.
  Hence the `|A|^2` Weyl matrices are orthogonal and form a basis of
  `End_C(C^A)`.  The matrix-unit proof then gives one simple module of
  dimension `|A|`; polar decomposition gives unitary intertwiners unique up to
  `U(1)`.  This is uniqueness after the named `iota`, not a canonical choice
  of a model-independent vector space.

- **Fixed-level tensor calculation.** The theorem uses the central product,
  not the Cartesian product of phase groups.  The external tensor of two
  `iota`-central models descends through the anti-diagonal centre and again has
  central character `iota`.  No claim that a fixed central-character
  representation sector is internally tensor closed appears in the artifact.

- **Ring convention and pushout.** If `chi_b=1`, then `bR` lies in the kernel
  of the generating character, hence `b=0`; cardinality gives
  `R ~= (R,+)^vee`.  The pushout map has kernel exactly
  `{(t,0,0):psi(t)=1}` and sends the raw cocycle `ab'` to `psi(ab')`.
  For `R=F_4`, the raw group has order `64`, the trace character has additive
  kernel of order `2`, and the pushout has order `32=2*4^2`.  This confirms why
  a raw-group identification would be false.  The recovered operator is
  exactly `Z(-b)X(a)`, including characteristic two.

- **Strict normalizer and Fourier lift.** Recomputing conjugation yields

      T W(a,chi) T^(-1)
        = q(a)/(B_q(a,a)(chi B_q(a,-))(f^(-1)t))
          W(f(a),(chi B_q(a,-)) o f^(-1)).

  Conversely normalization forces the underlying permutation to be affine and
  the phase ratio to have bicharacter polarization.  Fourier covariance has
  the stated signs, and `r_A(a,chi)=chi(a)` satisfies the lift equation because
  both sides reduce to `chi(a)chi(b)eta(b)`.  Full Fourier support for
  `|A|>1` proves it cannot be the realization of a strict pointed map; a
  self-Fourier endomorphism still needs a named self-duality.

- **Level one.** `exp(A)|1` forces `A=0`; this does not constrain the rank of
  an unrelated ordinary pointed-set F1 module.
