## Objections

### 1. MAJOR — `SW_fin(A_*)` is not closed under the displayed tensor at the stated generality

**(a) Exact location.** `DEFINITIONS-PROPOSED.md`, D1221--D1222, especially
the assertion that `direct-sum mod_fd-A_n` is a category with the displayed
induction tensor; `UNIVERSAL-CATEGORY.md` `<1>6.<2>3`--`<2>7`.  The latter
checks the right action and associativity but never proves that the induced
module remains finite-dimensional.  D1223's C*-version also needs the
structural maps `mu_(m,n)` to be star homomorphisms, not merely unital algebra
homomorphisms, in order that `mu(e tensor f)` be a self-adjoint projection.

**(b) Independent counterexample.**  Let
`A_n=C[x_(ij):1<=i<j<=n]`, with `A_0=A_1=C`, and let `mu_(m,n)` include the
variables internal to the first and second consecutive blocks.  The two
three-block maps agree, so this is a multiplicative sequence in D1221's sense.
For the one-dimensional right `A_1` modules `M=N=C`, however,

    M star N = C tensor_C A_2 = C[x_(12)],

which is not finite-dimensional and hence is not an object of
`mod_fd-A_2`.  Separately, if C*-algebras are connected by a non-star unital
algebra map, the image of a self-adjoint projection need not be self-adjoint.

**(c) FIX DEMAND.**  Add the hypothesis that every induction functor preserves
finite dimension (finite-dimensional `A_n` suffices here), and require star
structural maps in the C*-version.

**(d) SURVIVING WEAKER STATEMENT.**  For the positive Hecke sequence all
`H_n(q)` are finite-dimensional and the block maps are star maps, so the
algebraic finite-module/finite-projective strong monoidal equivalence used by
`F1-LIM-UNIV` and `F1-LIM-DM` survives unchanged.

### 2. MAJOR — D1236 does not actually type a rigid extension or comparison of `U_q`

necessary, and `<1>5.<2>5`--`<2>6`.  Also, the non-rigidity proof at

**(b) Independent counterexample.**  Literal D1236 is satisfied by taking
`R=FinHilb`, naming its unit object as `x`, taking `x^vee=x`, and using the
ordinary one-dimensional cup and cap.  Yet there is no fully faithful functor
from `U_q` carrying its generator to this `x`: already
`End_Uq(x^tensor 3)=H_3(q)` has dimension six while `End_R(x^tensor 3)=C`.
Thus a category merely “containing its chosen generator” need not contain,
receive, or quotient `U_q`.  For the omitted mixed-degree case in the
non-rigidity proof, if `y=direct-sum_k y_k`, every summand of `x tensor y` has
degree `1+k>0`, so every map to the unit is still zero; the conclusion is true
but the written quantifier is incomplete.

**(c) FIX DEMAND.**  Put in the datum a named strong monoidal functor
`i:U_q->R`, state whether it is fully faithful or a quotient/comparison, require
the cups/caps to dualize `i(x)`, and state the additive/Karoubi closure in which
duals of sums and retracts are formed.

**(d) SURVIVING WEAKER STATEMENT.**  `U_q` is not rigid, and at `q=1` the
Schur--Weyl **quotient image** generated by `V` has the concrete rigid unitary

### 3. MAJOR — the expectation bound is asserted beyond the proof's hypothesis
