# A positive conditional boundary for marked Frobenius orbits

Status: SKETCH for FRL-ORBIT and FRL-POS. These are structured derivations
with exact finite falsifiers, awaiting independent capped review. No PROVED
promotion is made in this session. Definitions: D1331--D1332. Existing inputs:
D1301--D1302, FRB-TRACE and FRB-FROB. Author: root Codex runtime.

This is a selected observable family with named orbit origins. It is not a
construction of the full p-to-one arithmetic amplitude category. The formal
variable t continues p, with extension degree r fixed; no prime sequence
converges to one and no field with noninteger cardinality is asserted.

Source comparison: Hyde, *Cyclotomic factors of necklace polynomials*,
arXiv:1811.08601v2, introduction p. 1, defines the necklace polynomial
M_d(t)=c_d(t)/d and its finite-field interpretation. The elementary counts,
positivity, matrix comparison and conditional normalization below are
derived here. Local source: refs/frobenius-boundary/1811.08601/paper.txt.
Nothing below relies on a normal basis theorem or odd characteristic.

## 1. FRL-ORBIT: exact periods, matrix blocks and physical comparison

**ASSUME** D1331 at t=p with E=F_(p^r).
**PROVE** c_d(p) is the number of elements of Frobenius period exactly d,
for d|r, and R_(p,r) is a faithful unital star representation with

    R_(p,r)(u_r)=U_E,
    Tr(R_(p,r)(a))/p^r=theta_(r,p)(a).

<1>1. **ASSUME** d|r. **PROVE** exactly p^d elements of E are fixed by
sigma_E^d.
  <2>1. The polynomial X^(p^r)-X has all elements of E as roots by
  FRB-TRACE, foundation.md <1>1; its degree is p^r, so it splits completely.
  **BY** that claim and the polynomial root bound derived there.
  <2>2. Modulo X^(p^d)-X, iteration of X^(p^d)=X gives X^(p^r)=X.
  Thus X^(p^d)-X divides X^(p^r)-X. Its derivative is -1, so its roots
  are distinct, and all lie in E by <2>1. There are exactly p^d of them.
  **BY** polynomial division and the formal derivative; a repeated linear
  factor forces the derivative to vanish at that root by the product rule.
  <2>3. These roots are exactly sigma_E^d-fixed labels by D1302.
  **QED** <1>1 by <2>1--<2>2.

<1>2. **ASSUME** sigma_E has order r. **PROVE** the orbit counts.
  <2>1. The least positive period of any point divides every positive
  exponent fixing that point: divide the exponent by the period, leaving
  a remainder which would otherwise be a smaller positive period.
  In particular every period divides r by FRB-FROB.
  **BY** the integer division algorithm and D1302.
  <2>2. If A_d counts exact period d, <1>1 gives
  p^e=sum_(d|e) A_d for every e|r. For any n, sum_(b|n) mu(b) is one
  for n=1 and zero otherwise: nonzero summands correspond to subsets of
  distinct prime factors, and their sum is (1-1)^k.
  **BY** D1331 and expansion of this finite product.
  <2>3. Substitute <2>2 into sum_(e|d) mu(d/e)p^e, interchange the
  finite divisor sums, and apply that divisor identity. Only A_d remains.
  Thus A_d=c_d(p), and the number of length-d orbits is c_d(p)/d.
  **BY** D1331 and the displayed finite sum computation.
  <2>4. **QED** the count. Positivity of every c_d(p), needed for
  faithfulness, is supplied without circularity by Section 2 below.

<1>3. **ASSUME** the origins in D1331. **PROVE** the representation and
trace statements.
  <2>1. The orbit subspaces are pairwise orthogonal coordinate subspaces
  whose direct sum is H_E. On a length-d orbit, its marked basis identifies
  the action with the usual M_d action. Direct sums of copies preserve
  multiplication, identity and adjoints.
  **BY** D1301's orthonormal basis and D1331.
  <2>2. Every d|r occurs by Section 2. A nonzero block matrix acts
  nontrivially on one of its copies, proving faithfulness. On each orbit
  Frobenius advances the marked basis by one, which is S_d.
  **BY** <2>1, D1302 and Section 2 <1>2.
  <2>3. A block occurs c_d(p)/d times. Its ordinary trace contribution
  is (c_d(p)/d)Tr(a_d)=c_d(p)tr_d(a_d). Divide by p^r and sum.
  **BY** <1>2, D1332 and direct summation of diagonal entries.
  <2>4. The origin test for period d is the sum of |b_O><b_O| over
  length-d orbits. Multiplying it on the left by U_E^i and on the right
  by U_E^(-j), 0<=i,j<d, gives exactly R_(p,r)(|i><j|_d). Thus the
  physical algebra is generated by actual Frobenius and these marked
  coordinate tests; the matrix units were not adjoined independently.
  **BY** D1331, D1302 and the basis action on each orbit. The reverse
  containment follows because both U_E and the origin tests are images
  of elements of O_r under <2>1--<2>2.
  <2>5. **QED** FRL-ORBIT by <1>1--<1>3 and Section 2 positivity.

<1>4. **ASSUME** a second set of orbit origins. **PROVE** the two
physical representations are unitarily conjugate, with U_E fixed.
  <2>1. In each orbit the origin changes by a unique power of Frobenius.
  The resulting orbitwise change of basis is a power of S_d. Direct-sum
  these powers to get a unitary on H_E, and conjugate every matrix block.
  **BY** D1331 and the definition of an orbit.
  <2>2. The change commutes with U_E because powers of a cycle commute.
  **QED** by <2>1. The embedded algebra and its marked rank-one tests can
  change; this is a named unitary comparison, not choice independence.

## 2. FRL-POS: positivity for all t>1 and the conditional endpoint

**ASSUME** D1331--D1332, with r>=2.
**PROVE** theta_(r,t) is a faithful normalized positive trace for t>1;
omega_(r,t) extends continuously to the displayed faithful positive trace
at t=1, on the fixed algebra O_r^+. In particular no arbitrary extra
matrix factor is needed to obtain the positive endpoint blocks.

<1>1. **ASSUME** d>1 and h>0, so t=exp(h).
**PROVE** c_d(t)>0 and c_d'(1)=phi(d).
  <2>1. Expand the finitely many exponentials in D1331:

      c_d(exp h)=sum_(k>=0) h^k/k! sum_(e|d) mu(d/e)e^k.

  This series converges absolutely, since it is a finite sum of convergent
  exponential series. Its k=0 term is zero by Section 1 <1>2.<2>2.
  **BY** D1331 and the exponential power series.
  <2>2. For k>=1 the inner sum factors as

      J_k(d)=d^k product_(ell prime, ell|d)(1-ell^(-k))>0.

  Expand the product over prime subsets; its term indexed by a squarefree
  divisor b is mu(b)(d/b)^k. This is exactly the inner divisor sum.
  **BY** D1331's mu and finite distributivity.
  <2>3. For k=1 the product counts numbers from 1 through d not divisible
  by any prime factor of d, by inclusion-exclusion. This is phi(d).
  Therefore c_d(exp h)=phi(d)h+O(h^2), giving c_d'(1)=phi(d).
  **BY** D1331's phi, <2>1--<2>2 and exp(h)=1+h+O(h^2).
  <2>4. Every term for k>=1 is positive; the first alone is nonzero.
  Hence c_d(t)>0 for all t>1. Also c_1(t)=t>0 directly.
  **QED** <1>1 by <2>1--<2>3.

<1>2. **ASSUME** r>=2. **PROVE** normalization and extension.
  <2>1. The finite divisor inversion in Section 1 gives the polynomial
  identity sum_(d|r)c_d(t)=t^r. Removing c_1(t)=t gives t^r-t.
  Differentiating at one gives sum_(d|r,d>1)phi(d)=r-1.
  **BY** Section 1 <1>2.<2>3 and <1>1.
  <2>2. The weights c_d(t)/t^r are positive and sum to one for t>1.
  A positive weighted sum of normalized matrix traces is positive and
  faithful: on a*a its summands are positive multiples of sums of squared
  entry moduli. A zero sum forces every entry of every block to vanish.
  **BY** <1>1, <2>1 and Tr(a*a)=sum_(i,j)|a_ij|^2.
  <2>3. On the active blocks divide by w_r(t)=(t^r-t)/t^r>0.
  Numerator c_d(t) and denominator t^r-t have simple zeros at one,
  with derivatives phi(d) and r-1. Their quotient extends continuously
  with value phi(d)/(r-1)>0.
  **BY** <1>1, D1332 and cancellation of the polynomial factor t-1.
  <2>4. The extended weights still sum to one by <2>1 and are all
  positive. The squared-entry proof in <2>2 gives faithful positivity.
  **QED** FRL-POS by <2>1--<2>4.

<1>3. **ASSUME** the full ambient trace is specialized without conditioning.
**PROVE** its trace-null quotient retains only the d=1 block.
  <2>1. At t=1, c_1(1)=1 and c_d(1)=0 for d>1. Thus theta_(r,1)
  evaluates only a_1. Its null ideal on a*a is exactly O_r^+.
  **BY** D1332 and Section 1's divisor identity.
  <2>2. In the quotient u_r becomes S_1=1. In particular the same
  construction exhibits both ordinary collapse and conditional survival.
  **QED** by <2>1. Conditioning changes the reference state prescription.

## 3. Observable survival and the smallest examples

**ASSUME** D1332 and integer k.
**PROVE** the exact conditional moments and a state/effect distinction:

    omega_(r,t)((u_r^+)^k)=(t^gcd(r,k)-t)/(t^r-t),  t>1,
    omega_(r,1)((u_r^+)^k)=(gcd(r,k)-1)/(r-1).

<1>1. A cyclic shift S_d^k has normalized trace one if d divides k,
and zero otherwise; it fixes every basis index or none. Summing precisely
those c_d(t) with d|gcd(r,k), excluding d=1, gives the numerator above.
**BY** D1331 and Section 2 <1>2.<2>1. Use gcd(r,0)=r.
<1>2. Take the quotient of derivatives at one as in Section 2. In
particular omega_(r,1)(u_r^+)=0 and
omega_(r,1)((u_r^+-e_r)^*(u_r^+-e_r))=2.
**BY** <1>1 and unitarity of every S_d; expand 2e_r-u_r^+-(u_r^+)^*.
<1>3. On any block d>1 prepare density |0><0| (zero on other blocks)
and test effect |0><0|. Its probability is one initially and zero after
conjugation by u_r^+. Also |0><0|S_d != S_d|0><0| on |d-1>.
**BY** D1331 and ordinary matrix multiplication on the named basis.
This proves operational nontriviality in the specified complex CP envelope;
the selected matrix tests are part of the marked comparison data.
<1>4. On the block d=r, the successive conjugates of |0><0| are the r
distinct coordinate projections. Hence both u_r^+ and its conjugation
channel have exact order r; all block lengths divide r.
**BY** D1331, <1>3 and the retained d=r block. **QED** by <1>1--<1>4.

For r=2, O_2^+=M_2, omega_(2,t)=tr_2 for every t>=1, and u_2^+=S_2.
At p=2 the physical active orbit in F4 is (alpha,alpha^2), giving the
whole two-dimensional corner. The actual Frobenius exchanges these kets.
The ambient active mass is (t-1)/t; it vanishes while the conditional qubit
state and flip remain constant. The cyclic operation has order two for a
reason fixed by arithmetic extension degree, not by a varying phase level.

For r=4, O_4^+=M_2 direct-sum M_4, and the conditional weights are

    lambda_(4,2)(t)=1/(t^2+t+1),
    lambda_(4,4)(t)=t(t+1)/(t^2+t+1).

They are (1/7,6/7) at t=2 and (1/3,2/3) at one. The second Frobenius
moment is respectively 1/7 and 1/3. These rational functions follow by
factoring c_2=t^2-t, c_4=t^4-t^2 and t^4-t. They agree with the exact
F16 census: two period-two labels and twelve period-four labels.

## 4. What has actually been constructed

On any compact interval [1,T], C([1,T],O_r^+) is an explicit continuous
matrix-algebra section family with continuous faithful normalized traces
omega_(r,t). At every prime t=p in that interval it compares to the marked
orbit observable subalgebra and its conditioned ordinary physical trace.
The trace formula extends to all t>=1 by the same definition.

The algebra is not the full End(H_E), nor the commutant of Frobenius:
the rank-one test above does not commute with it. Equal-period orbit copies
have been identified using origins, while their physical multiplicities
remain in the trace. Arbitrary field permutations and arithmetic gates need
not preserve that identification. The construction is a positive answer
for this explicitly selected family, with the full mixed arithmetic limit
still open. Independent tensor and inclusion are addressed in the companion
shard orbit-composition.md, without silently strengthening this comparison.
