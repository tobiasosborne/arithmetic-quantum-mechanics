# SUMMARY — exact affine-relation and compact-data lane

Model: `gpt-5.6-sol`, reasoning setting `xhigh`.

The lane supplies a standalone exact checker proposal for SP-LREL and the
separately formulated SP-COMPACT claim.  `EXPECTATIONS.md` was written and
reported before implementation.  The lane read no relations prover artifact
and made no trunk edits, stages or commits.

G1 enumerates the complete zero/one-register Hom-sets over F2 and F3.  The
counts are `(2,7,7,61)` and `(2,13,13,361)`, including explicit empty arrows,
all states/effects and nonfunctional affine relations.  Its actual wrong-sign
catalog retains the characteristic-two accident and loses the diagonal over
F3, so the sign red is genuinely odd-characteristic-sensitive.

G2 checks every 4,705 F2 and 140,101 F3 composable ordered pair.  Sparse
point-set existential joins are compared with a separately implemented exact
linear-equation solver and projected-dimension oracle.  Registered horizontal
line witnesses exercise a nontransverse fiber of size q and a disjoint affine
composite that is empty.  G3 checks exhaustive identities, converse dagger and
dagger composition, exhaustive associativity for 290,794 F2 triples, and
4,096 deterministic typed F3 triples.

G4 checks 5,929 exhaustive F2 and 4,096 typed F3 tensor pairs, independent
coordinate reordering through a Boolean-relation-matrix Kronecker oracle,
affine typing, tensor/dagger compatibility, the
actual zero unit, strict associativity, swap naturality/involution, the
symmetric hexagon and selected interchange.  G5 enumerates all 24 and 216
affine symplectic plane arrows, checks faithful Lagrangian graphs, all 47,232
graph compositions, graph daggers and 4,672 direct-sum/tensor comparisons.

G6--G7 follow canonical D1714 exactly.  The flattened tuple model separately
constructs its graph associator, unitors and swap, then composes those arrows
in both typed snakes, the dagger equation, unname, lambda_0-transported scalar
tensor and the closed loop.  All 466 small relations are named and unnamed,
including every empty case.  The loop is the true relation, while its q^2
middle witnesses are deliberately not retained as a scalar weight.

After the single checker repair wave, all fourteen actual-data mutations exit
`1` at their registered gates.  They
cover source form sign, empty-arrow loss, universal middle quantification,
dagger and tensor coordinate order, a nonzero tensor unit, lost affine graph
translation, compact dual and cup order, empty-name corruption, closed-loop
multiplicity and name order, plus literal compact-dagger swap omission and a
same-typed empty first-snake cap.  The compact modes now fail at distinct
subchecks G6a--G6d.  A temporary disabled G6c comparison made the
`dagger-swap` red survive with exit `0`; restoring it returned the red to
exit `1`.
Invalid usage exits `2`, and both ordinary and optimized green runs pass.

The checker uses exact prime-field tuples, sparse frozen point sets, cached
catalogs/composites and exact modular row reduction.  Its evidence is finite
and negative-binding only.  It does not prove SP-LREL or SP-COMPACT for every
finite field or arbitrary rank, and it says nothing about normalized quantum
semantics, amplitudes, stabilizer equivalence or Choi operators.  `PATCH.md`
gives string-anchored integration steps while retaining both claim statuses at
`SKETCH` pending their proof/review loop.
