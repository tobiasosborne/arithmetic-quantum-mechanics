# DG-CHAR2 planning summary

The smallest useful carrier is an un-split defect-lift groupoid with arrows
`(g,r)` and no chosen section, not D1701 affine arrows plus an assumed
section. Its kernel retains
translation-like phase characters; its exact algebra action and projective
unitary give phase-independent channels suitable for a later global datum.

The candidate separates an all-rank characteristic-two Weyl theorem from the
lift/functor theorem. It names beta, coordinates, models, cochains and unitary
phases; specifies composition, inverses, unit and tensor; retains the central
`U(1)` implementer extension; and makes no splitting claim.

GH08 uses a raw W2(k)-central Heisenberg group, kernel `Hom(V,R)`, extra Witt
and Lagrangian-splitting data, and a separate central `mu_4` AMp extension.
Applying its central character may map into the proposed phase-level lift, but
kernel, image and finite-central-extension comparison remain calculations.

F1-REAL/F1-RING supply the models; L-TRIV supplies individual `mu_4` defect
cochains; WH-WEIL-a/c/d are rank-one regressions. F1-FUNCT must be added
explicitly for fixed-level tensor coherence. No completion-lane artifact,
canonical file, status or Git state was touched.
