# Relation-lift planning summary

Planning model: `gpt-5.6-sol`, reasoning `xhigh`.

`GENERATOR-MATCH.md` defines a precise odd-prime term fragment on the named
prime-field arithmetic atom and matches zero/basis preparations, rephased
Weyl translations, positive Fourier, controlled addition, quadratic phase,
swap/tensor, coefficient scalars and compact cups/caps to actual stabilizer
representatives and affine-relation labels.

The coefficient audit keeps the arithmetic source over
`R_p=Q(zeta_p,sqrt(p))`; arbitrary complex stabilizer scalars are excluded.
The table separately tracks arithmetic source equality, literal realized-map
or CP equality, and all-invertible-scalar projective equality. Extension-field
transfers, codes, Frobenius and higher multiplication gates remain outside,
so the complete arithmetic source is not identified with pure stabilizer
theory.

`TARGET.md` proposes one bounded positive generator comparison with explicit
dependencies and falsifiers. Its cup/cap calculation records why the current
targets are insufficient: unnormalized compact data have loop `p` and exact
snakes, whereas physical normalization has loop `1` and snake scalar
`p^(-1)`.

The proposed later lift therefore needs Hermitian one-dimensional amplitude
lines with coherent composition, tensor and dagger, actual cup/cap elements,
norms for probabilities, and an `R_p`-form on the matched fragment. D1715's
semantic lines are a benchmark, not the geometric definition, and no
semantic-kernel quotient is claimed canonical.

`PATCH.md` gives future registration anchors. This lane contains planning
only: no proof, admission, canonical/status/Git change, completion re-review,
characteristic-two assumption, global construction, modular claim or
spectral assertion.
