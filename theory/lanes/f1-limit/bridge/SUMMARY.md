# Vector, Fourier and type-C bridge — lane summary

## Exact vector/Weyl identification

For `L=F_Q^n`, the arithmetic mirabolic algebra is concretely

`R_X(L)=End_(GL(L) semidirect L) C[Fl(L) times L]`.

Writing an invariant kernel as
`K((F,x),(F',x'))=f(F,F',x'-x)` turns operator composition exactly into
Rosso's equation-(6) convolution.  Hilbert adjoint is Rosso's orbit
anti-involution, and normalized operator trace is the identity-orbit
coefficient.  This fixes representation, star and trace, rather than using the
abstract q-rook comparison.

The embedded Hecke algebra acts as `A_w tensor I`.  Rosso's vector idempotent

`e=(T_0+I)/Q`

is exactly the context-controlled translation average `C_1^X`.  Rosso's
generation theorem therefore proves that the Hecke transitions plus this one
controlled Weyl projection generate the full affine commutant.

Finite Fourier transform and dual-flag reversal
`D(F)_j=U_(n-j)^perp` satisfy

`W C_i^X W^*=C_(n-i)^Z`,
`W(A_w tensor I)W^*=A_(w_0ww_0) tensor I`.

Here `0<=i<=n`; in particular `C_0^Z=I_(flags) tensor |0><0|`, and in rank
one the nontrivial generator is the image `C_1^X |-> C_0^Z`.

Consequently the existing joint algebra generated by all `A_w tensor I` and
controlled `P^Z_U` constraints is exactly the Fourier image of the mirabolic
commutant.  The comparison is natural for linear isomorphisms.  Its phase
character and implementing Fourier unitary remain explicit; a same-register
unitary names a self-duality `L~=L^vee`.  This sector does not contain the full
noncommuting Weyl matrix algebra.

## Positive trace and the actual q=1 quotient

Fix relative position `w` and set `i prec_w j` when
`i<j` and `w^(-1)(i)<w^(-1)(j)`.  Vector suborbits of
`B intersect wBw^(-1)` are indexed by antichains `A`, and their affine orbital
valencies are

`k_(w,A)(q)=q^(ell(w)+|down_w(A)|-|A|)(q-1)^|A|`.

Orbit adjacencies have diagonal trace Gram matrix with these entries.  Rosso's
polynomial structure constants and equality at infinitely many prime-power
fibres extend the formula to every real `q>1`, proving a faithful positive
trace and a regular finite C*-realization.  Polynomial presentation alone
would not prove this.

Deleting all nonempty-antichain coefficients is the trace-preserving UCP
conditional expectation `R_n(q)->H_n(q)`.  It retracts the trace-preserving
Hecke inclusion, so every Hecke state, effect and retained Kraus process has an
unchanged positive mirabolic lift.

At `q=1`, precisely the nonempty-antichain basis vectors have zero trace norm.
Their span is the GNS ideal, and

`R_n(1)/N_(tau_1) ~= H_n(1)=C[S_n]`.

Coefficient deletion becomes this multiplicative quotient.  The algebraic
endpoint still contains the null ideal.  In rank one its two projection weights
are `1/q,(q-1)/q`; the second-sector density `q(1-e)/(q-1)` is normalized for
`q>1` but has a pole at one.  Thus the reference GNS quotient does not license
erasing singular state families.

## Type-C and affine composition correspondences

For symplectic spaces of ranks `m,n`, decomposable isotropic flags are exactly

`X_C(V) times X_C(W) times Sh(m,n)`.

In the local-symmetry commutant their projection `p` has corner

`p End_(Sp(V) times Sp(W))(K_(V orthogonal-sum W)) p`
` ~= A_C(V) tensor A_C(W) tensor M_(Sh(m,n))(C)`.

Compression `a|->pap` from the full composite type-C context algebra is UCP
and normalized-trace preserving.  If `r=rank(p)/dim(K)`, scaled full-group
averaging `x|->r^(-1)E_Sp(x)` is its trace-adjoint UCP preparation.  Canonical
bijections between nested binary shuffles and ternary shuffles prove exact
associativity of both channels.

The block `B_m times B_n` is proper of full rank in `B_(m+n)`, hence is not
parabolic.  At one, every signed permutation nevertheless factors into a
block signed permutation and an unsigned shuffle, giving the exact group
algebra endpoint.  The finite-field correspondence and endpoint inclusion are
proved; a generic type-C Hecke tensor map is not asserted.

The same decomposable-flag projection, now retaining every vector label,
gives trace-adjoint UCP assembly maps for arithmetic affine-vector commutants.
This supplies arithmetic mirabolic composition with shuffle coherence without
claiming a positive generic tensor law.
